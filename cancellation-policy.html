Cancellation Policy

This policy explains the 24-hour cancellation rule, lesson-credit returns, teacher cancellations, free trials and missed lessons.

Last updated: 28 July 2026 polyglot.school.admin@gmail.com
On this page 1. Policy overview 2. Student cancellation 3. Teacher cancellation 4. Free-trial cancellation 5. Late arrival and no-show 6. Rescheduling 7. Confirmation and Meet 8. Exceptional circumstances 9. Monetary refunds 10. Contact
01

Policy overview

A lesson is cancelled only when Polyglot successfully records its status as cancelled. Closing the browser, sending a chat message or not attending does not by itself cancel a booking.

The cancellation deadline is calculated from the lesson’s scheduled start time. The platform stores lesson times with time-zone information; users should check the displayed local date and time carefully.

02

Cancellation by a student

At least 24 hours before the lesson

If the student cancels a paid lesson at least 24 hours before its scheduled start, the booking is cancelled and one paid lesson credit is returned to the student’s Polyglot balance.

Less than 24 hours before the lesson

If the student cancels with less than 24 hours remaining, the booking is cancelled but the paid lesson credit is not returned. The reserved time cannot usually be offered to another student at short notice.

A returned lesson credit may be used for a new booking. It is not an automatic cash refund to PayPal.

03

Cancellation by a teacher

If a teacher cancels a paid lesson, the booking is cancelled and one lesson credit is returned to the student’s balance, regardless of how much time remains before the scheduled start.

A cancelled lesson does not generate teacher earnings. Repeated, unreasonable teacher cancellations may lead to profile review, temporary restriction or removal from the platform.

04

Free-trial cancellation

The free trial lasts 20 minutes and is limited to one eligible trial per student.

  • If the student cancels at least 24 hours before the trial, the trial entitlement should remain available for another eligible booking.
  • If the student cancels with less than 24 hours remaining or does not attend, the trial may be treated as used.
  • If the teacher cancels, the student’s eligible trial entitlement is restored.

If the dashboard does not show the expected trial status after a cancellation, contact support before making another booking.

05

Late arrival and no-show

A student or teacher who expects to be late should notify the other participant through Polyglot chat as soon as possible. A late arrival does not automatically extend the lesson beyond its scheduled end time.

If a student does not attend and has not completed a qualifying cancellation, the lesson credit is not returned. If a teacher does not attend, the student should contact support so the booking can be reviewed and the credit restored where appropriate.

06

Rescheduling a lesson

Polyglot does not guarantee that a booking can be moved directly. Rescheduling normally means cancelling the original booking and booking another available time.

The same 24-hour rule applies to the cancellation step. Another time with the same teacher is not guaranteed and remains subject to availability.

07

Cancellation confirmation and Google Meet

After cancellation, the booking should move from upcoming lessons to lesson history and display a cancelled status. Polyglot may also post a system message in the conversation.

Where a Google Calendar event and Google Meet link were created, Polyglot will attempt to cancel or update the event and mark the link as inactive. Users must not use or distribute a link from a cancelled booking.

If the booking still appears scheduled after you cancel, refresh the page once and contact support with the booking details if the status does not change.

08

Exceptional circumstances

We may review documented exceptional circumstances such as a serious medical emergency, bereavement, widespread service outage or a verified platform failure. Any discretionary exception is assessed individually and does not create an automatic entitlement for future cancellations.

Contact us as soon as reasonably possible and include the booking date, teacher name and a short explanation. Provide only information reasonably needed for the review.

09

Lesson credits and monetary refunds

The rules above determine whether a lesson credit returns to the Polyglot balance. They do not replace statutory withdrawal rights or other mandatory consumer remedies.

Requests to reverse a lesson-package payment to PayPal are handled under the Refund Policy. A user should not open a duplicate payment dispute while the same matter is actively being resolved unless necessary to preserve a legal or payment-provider deadline.
