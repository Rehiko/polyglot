02

Information we collect

The information we collect depends on whether you use Polyglot as a student, teacher or administrator.

  • full name;
  • email address;
  • account identifier;
  • account role;
  • authentication and account-security information;
  • profile image, where provided; and
  • account creation and update dates.

Teacher information

  • biography and profile headline;
  • languages and native language;
  • teaching methods and student levels;
  • experience information;
  • weekly availability and time zone;
  • profile approval status; and
  • lesson and earnings records.

Student and lesson information

  • lesson balance and purchase history;
  • free-trial status;
  • teacher selections;
  • booking dates, times and status;
  • lesson-credit transactions;
  • cancellation history; and
  • homework and learning-material activity.

Communication and uploaded content

  • chat messages;
  • message timestamps and read status;
  • uploaded files and filenames;
  • learning materials;
  • homework assignments and submissions;
  • teacher feedback; and
  • Google Meet information connected to lessons.

Technical information

  • browser and device information;
  • IP address where recorded by our providers;
  • authentication session information;
  • time-zone information;
  • security and error logs; and
  • information necessary to prevent fraud and misuse.
03

How information is collected

We receive personal information:

  • directly from you when you register, edit a profile, purchase lessons, book a lesson or contact us;
  • when students and teachers communicate, upload files or complete lessons;
  • automatically through authentication, security and platform functionality;
  • from PayPal when a payment is created, confirmed, rejected or refunded;
  • from Google when a calendar event or Google Meet meeting is created; and
  • from Supabase and other service providers used to operate the platform.
04

How and why we use information

We use personal information to:

  • create and protect user accounts;
  • identify students, teachers and administrators;
  • review and approve teacher profiles;
  • display approved teacher profiles;
  • process lesson-package purchases;
  • maintain student lesson balances;
  • create, manage and cancel bookings;
  • generate Google Meet links and calendar events;
  • enable chat and file sharing;
  • provide materials and homework functionality;
  • calculate and record teacher earnings;
  • provide customer support;
  • prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents;
  • comply with legal and accounting obligations; and
  • improve the stability and usability of Polyglot.

We process information where it is necessary to provide the requested service, fulfil an agreement with the user, comply with a legal obligation, protect the platform and its users, or where the user has provided valid consent.

05

Payments and financial information

Lesson-package payments are processed by PayPal. Polyglot does not store your PayPal password or complete payment-card details.

We may receive and store:

  • PayPal order and capture identifiers;
  • purchased package identifier;
  • number of purchased lessons;
  • payment amount and currency;
  • payment status;
  • payment timestamps; and
  • information necessary to investigate a dispute or refund.

PayPal processes payment information under its own privacy policy and legal obligations.

06

Chat, files, materials and Google Meet

Students and teachers who have a lesson relationship may communicate through Polyglot chat. Messages and files are stored so participants can access the conversation and learning history.

Teachers may upload textbooks, lesson materials, homework and feedback. Students may upload completed homework and chat attachments.

Files must not contain unlawful content, malware or unnecessary sensitive personal information. Users should not upload identification documents, passwords, complete payment-card information or confidential information that is not needed for a lesson.

Google Calendar may be used to create a lesson event and Google Meet link. The student’s and teacher’s names, email addresses, lesson time and meeting information may be sent to Google for this purpose.

Google processes this information under its own privacy terms. Polyglot does not record Google Meet video or audio unless users are separately informed and a lawful basis exists.

07

Cookies, authentication and local storage

Polyglot and Supabase use browser storage and similar technologies to maintain authentication sessions, remember essential settings and protect user accounts.

Essential authentication storage is required for signed-in areas of the platform to work. Removing it may sign you out or prevent dashboard features from operating.

We will provide additional notice or request consent before using non-essential analytics or advertising technologies where required by applicable law.

08

Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only when necessary with:

  • students and teachers participating in the same lesson relationship;
  • authorised Polyglot administrators and support staff;
  • Supabase for authentication, database and file storage;
  • PayPal for payment processing;
  • Google for calendar events and Google Meet links;
  • GitHub Pages and related infrastructure used to host the public website;
  • email providers used for authentication and service messages; and
  • public authorities where disclosure is legally required.

Some providers may process information outside Ukraine. Where required, we take reasonable steps to use providers offering appropriate contractual and technical safeguards.

09

How long we keep information

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including providing lessons, maintaining accounts, resolving disputes and complying with legal obligations.

  • Account and profile information is generally kept while the account remains active.
  • Booking, payment and accounting records may be kept for the period required by Ukrainian law.
  • Chat, files, materials and homework may remain available while the relevant accounts and learning relationship remain active.
  • Security logs may be kept for a limited period needed to investigate incidents and protect the platform.

Information may be deleted, anonymised or restricted when it is no longer required, unless continued retention is necessary under applicable law.

10

Data security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information, including:

  • authenticated user sessions;
  • role-based access restrictions;
  • Row Level Security database policies;
  • restricted private file-storage buckets;
  • server-side payment verification;
  • protected API credentials and secrets;
  • HTTPS connections; and
  • administrative access controls.

No online system can guarantee absolute security. Users must protect their passwords, devices and email accounts and notify us promptly about suspected unauthorised access.

11

Your privacy rights

Subject to the Law of Ukraine “On Personal Data Protection” and other applicable laws, you may have the right to:

  • know where and why your personal information is processed;
  • request access to your personal information;
  • request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • object to unlawful processing;
  • request deletion where there is no lawful reason to retain the information;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • receive information about parties who receive your information;
  • complain to the competent authority; and
  • seek protection against unlawful processing or accidental loss.

To exercise a right, email polyglot.school.admin@gmail.com . We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

Some information cannot be deleted immediately where it must be retained for legal, payment, accounting, fraud prevention or dispute-resolution purposes.

12

Children, policy changes and complaints

Polyglot is not intended for independent registration by children under 16. Where a child uses the service, a parent or legal guardian should provide the necessary authorisation and supervise the account.

We may update this Privacy Policy when the platform, service providers or legal requirements change. The latest version will display a revised update date.

Privacy questions or complaints should first be sent to polyglot.school.admin@gmail.com .

Users may also contact the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights regarding personal-data protection: official personal-data protection information .

This policy is governed by Ukrainian law, including the Law of Ukraine “On Personal Data Protection”.
