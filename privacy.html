Your privacy at Polyglot

Privacy Policy

This notice explains what personal data Polyglot collects, why it is used, who it may be shared with, and the rights available to you.

01

Who we are

Polyglot is an online language-learning platform that connects students with independent language teachers for lesson booking, online lessons, messaging, learning materials and homework.

The controller responsible for personal data processed through Polyglot is:

Controller: [Roman Hryniuk], trading as Polyglot

Postal address: [The LiffeyVale Nurseries, Naas, CO Kildare, Ireland]

Email: polyglot.school.admin@gmail.com

Country of establishment: Ireland

In this notice, “Polyglot”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to that controller.

02

Personal data we collect

Account and profile information

This may include your name, email address, account identifier, user role, profile photo and authentication information. Teacher profiles may also include a biography, languages, headline, teaching methods, experience, lesson duration, time zone and approval status.

Lesson, booking and account activity

We process lesson balances, free-trial status, teacher availability, bookings, lesson start and end times, cancellations, lesson status, attendance-related records and lesson credit transactions.

Messages, homework and learning materials

We process chat messages, read status, attachments, course materials, homework instructions, submissions, deadlines, teacher feedback and related file information when you use these features.

Payment and teacher earnings information

We keep payment records such as the package purchased, amount, currency, payment status and PayPal order or capture identifiers. Payment credentials and full card details are handled by PayPal and are not stored by Polyglot. We also keep teacher earnings and manual payout status records.

Google Calendar and Google Meet information

When a lesson is booked, we may create a calendar event and Google Meet link using a connected Google account. We process the event identifier, meeting link, lesson time and the information needed to invite the student and teacher.

Technical and security information

We may receive information such as browser and device details, IP address, authentication and security events, error information and timestamps when necessary to operate, protect and troubleshoot the service.

03

How we use personal data

We use personal data to:

  • create and secure student, teacher and administrator accounts;
  • display approved teacher profiles and available lesson times;
  • process lesson purchases and maintain lesson balances;
  • book, manage and cancel lessons;
  • create Google Calendar events and Google Meet links;
  • enable messages, attachments, homework and learning materials;
  • calculate teacher earnings and record manual payouts;
  • send account, security, booking and service-related emails;
  • provide support, investigate errors and prevent misuse;
  • comply with legal, tax, accounting and regulatory obligations; and
  • improve the reliability and usability of Polyglot.

Polyglot does not use personal data for solely automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

04

Legal bases for processing

Depending on the activity, we rely on one or more of the following:

  • Contract and steps before a contract: to register an account, purchase, book and provide lessons and platform features.
  • Legal obligation: to keep records and meet applicable tax, accounting, consumer-protection and regulatory duties.
  • Legitimate interests: to operate and secure the platform, prevent fraud, provide support, resolve disputes and improve the service, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Consent: where consent is required, including for optional technologies or communications. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing.
05

Who receives personal data

We share only the information reasonably necessary to provide and protect the service. Recipients may include:

  • Students and teachers: the people participating in a booking can receive relevant profile, lesson, chat, material and meeting information.
  • Supabase: authentication, database, file storage, real-time features and server-side functions.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting the public website.
  • PayPal: payment processing and fraud prevention.
  • Google: Google Calendar, Google Meet, Gmail SMTP and Google Fonts, as applicable.
  • Professional advisers and authorities: where reasonably necessary for legal, accounting, security or regulatory purposes.

These providers may process data under their own privacy notices in addition to their contractual duties to Polyglot.

We do not sell personal data.

06

International data transfers

Some service providers may process data outside Ireland or the European Economic Area. Where required, transfers are protected by an adequacy decision, standard contractual clauses or another lawful safeguard. You may contact us for more information about the safeguards relevant to your data.

07

How long we keep data

We keep personal data only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this notice. Account and learning information is generally retained while an account is active and for a reasonable period afterward to handle support, disputes, security and reactivation requests.

Payment, earnings, transaction and related business records may be kept longer where Irish tax, accounting or other law requires it. Backup copies may remain for a limited period before being overwritten. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it where reasonably possible.

08

How we protect data

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data. These include authenticated access, role-based permissions, database row-level security, restricted file-storage policies, server-side secrets and encrypted connections.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Please use a strong, unique password and contact us promptly if you believe your account has been compromised.

09

Your data-protection rights

Subject to the conditions in applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • access personal data we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • request deletion of your data;
  • restrict certain processing;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • receive certain data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; and
  • make a complaint to a supervisory authority.

To exercise a right, email polyglot.school.admin@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

In Ireland, you may also complain to the Data Protection Commission .

10

Children

Polyglot is not intended for independent registration by children under 16. If we offer the service to a child under 16 in Ireland and rely on consent for an online service, the consent of a parent or guardian will be required. Parents or guardians may contact us about a child’s data.

If you believe a child has created an account without the required permission, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.

11

Cookies, local storage and third-party technologies

Polyglot uses essential browser storage and similar technologies to keep users signed in, protect accounts and remember information needed for core platform functions. PayPal and Google features may also use cookies or similar technologies under their own notices.

Polyglot does not currently use advertising cookies or a separate analytics service. If this changes, this notice and any required consent controls will be updated before non-essential technologies are used.

12

Changes to this notice and how to contact us

We may update this notice when the service, providers or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. We will provide an additional notice where a change is material and the law requires it.

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to polyglot.school.admin@gmail.com.
