Refund Policy

This policy explains when a lesson credit is returned, when a monetary refund may be available and how to submit a request.

Last updated: 28 July 2026 polyglot.school.admin@gmail.com
On this page 1. Credits and money 2. Statutory withdrawal 3. Used lesson packages 4. Service problems 5. Payment errors 6. Refund processing 7. Requesting a refund 8. Cancellation form 9. Statutory rights
01

Lesson credits are different from money refunds

When a student cancels an eligible booking, Polyglot may return one lesson credit to the student’s platform balance. This lets the student book a new lesson, but it does not send money back to PayPal.

A monetary refund reverses all or part of a lesson-package payment to the original payment method. Monetary refunds are handled separately under this policy and applicable consumer law.

Booking cancellations are explained in the Cancellation Policy.

02

Statutory right to withdraw from an online purchase

Under Article 13 of the Law of Ukraine “On Consumer Protection”, a consumer may generally terminate an eligible distance contract within 14 days from its conclusion or from receiving the required confirmation, subject to the conditions and exceptions established by Ukrainian law.

To exercise that right, send a clear written statement before the deadline to polyglot.school.admin@gmail.com. You may use the model form below, but it is not mandatory.

If you request or use lessons during this period, that use may affect the availability or amount of a refund to the extent permitted by Ukrainian law. Lessons already completed and other services already supplied will be taken into account when we assess the request.

These rules do not affect remedies available when a service is faulty, unavailable or not supplied as agreed.

03

Unused and partially used lesson packages

Unused package

If no lesson credit from a package has been used, contact us promptly. We will review whether a statutory refund is required or whether a discretionary refund can be approved.

Partially used package

If one or more credits have been used to receive completed lessons, those lessons are normally treated as services already supplied. Where a refund is legally required or approved, we may deduct the lawful value of services already provided and explain the calculation.

Completed lessons

A completed lesson is not refundable merely because the student changed their mind or did not achieve a hoped-for result. This does not limit rights where the lesson or platform service failed to conform to the contract.

04

Teacher cancellation or service problem

If a teacher cancels a paid lesson, the platform restores the lesson credit so the student can rebook. If rebooking or restoring the service is not a suitable lawful remedy, contact us to request review of a monetary refund.

If a paid service is not supplied, is materially different from what was agreed or cannot be fixed within a reasonable time without significant inconvenience, you may be entitled to a full or proportionate refund under applicable law.

Provide the booking date, teacher name and a short explanation. Screenshots or relevant chat records may help us investigate.

05

Duplicate, incorrect or unauthorised payments

Contact us immediately if you see a duplicate charge, receive the wrong lesson package or do not recognise a payment. Include your account email, PayPal order or transaction identifier and the amount charged.

Do not email or send through chat your PayPal password, bank password, complete card number or security code. You may also need to notify PayPal or your payment provider about suspected unauthorised activity.

06

How approved refunds are processed

  • Where law requires a refund, it will be made without undue delay and no later than the applicable statutory deadline.
  • Refunds are normally returned through the original PayPal payment method unless another method is expressly agreed and legally permitted.
  • PayPal, the card issuer or bank may require additional processing time after Polyglot issues the refund.
  • Currency conversion differences or payment-provider fees outside Polyglot’s control may be governed by PayPal’s terms.
  • Refunded lesson credits will be removed from the student balance so they cannot also be used for bookings.
07

How to request a refund

Email polyglot.school.admin@gmail.com with the subject Refund request and include:

  • your full name and Polyglot account email;
  • the package and purchase date;
  • the PayPal order or capture identifier, if available;
  • the reason for the request; and
  • whether any lesson credits from the package have been used.

We may ask for reasonable information needed to verify the account, transaction and refund entitlement.

08
