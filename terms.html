The platform and user roles

Polyglot provides tools for discovering approved teachers, purchasing lesson credits, booking lessons, joining online meetings, messaging, sharing files, assigning homework and recording teacher earnings.

Students choose a teacher and an available time. Teachers control their profiles and working hours, provide lessons, communicate with booked students and may upload learning materials. Administrators may approve teacher profiles, provide support, manage platform records and record teacher payouts.

A teacher’s approval means the profile has passed Polyglot’s platform review. It is not a guarantee of a particular learning outcome, qualification, suitability or uninterrupted availability.

Bookings and lessons

A booking is confirmed when the platform records it as scheduled. One paid lesson credit is normally deducted when a paid lesson is booked.

  • A standard paid lesson lasts 60 minutes unless the booking clearly states otherwise.
  • Times are displayed using the relevant account and browser time-zone information.
  • Students and teachers should verify the displayed date, time and time zone before confirming.
  • Users need a suitable device, stable internet connection, audio and video capability where required.
  • Joining late does not automatically extend the scheduled end time.
  • Rescheduling normally requires cancellation of the original booking and creation of a new booking.

A Google Meet link may be generated after booking. The lesson remains confirmed while the meeting link is being prepared unless the platform displays an error or cancellation notice.
